Frequently asked questions

Clear expectations matter more than impressive claims.

Does the demo scan my live website?

No. The current prototype creates illustrative sample findings in your browser. It does not make network requests or verify anything about the URL.

Is this a replacement for a technical SEO audit?

No. The intended product is a practical, evidence-backed starting point. Technical conclusions should be validated with appropriate tools and expert review.

Can agencies use reports with their own clients?

That is a core use case to validate. Before offering white-label or client redistribution features, agree on scope, attribution, data handling and report quality.

What happens after the pilot?

Early customers help identify which checks and report sections are genuinely useful. Automation should follow repeated customer needs, not precede validation.
